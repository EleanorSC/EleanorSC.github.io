Course Overview

These tutorials introduce Prelims / first-year Psychology (PP/EP) students to the neural and physiological bases of cognition and behaviour. Teaching integrates cognitive neuroscience, neurophysiology and neuropsychology, with an emphasis on evaluating how experimental evidence supports theories of brain–behaviour relationships.

The course emphasises:

-Understanding the neural basis of cognition and behaviour -Linking neurophysiological mechanisms to psychological processes -Interpretation of neuropsychological and neuroimaging evidence -Critical evaluation of experimental evidence -Development of clear, evidence-based arguments -Preparation for Oxford Prelims examinations

Topics Covered:

-Neuronal signalling and synaptic transmission -Organisation and function of the nervous system -Canonical principles of cognitive neuroscience -Neural basis of learning and memory -Episodic memory and the hippocampal system -Language and the brain -Hemispheric specialisation and lateralisation -Neuropsychology and lesion studies -Neuroimaging and experimental approaches to brain function -Relationships between brain, cognition and behaviour

Recommended Textbooks:

Gazzaniga, Ivry & Mangun — Cognitive Neuroscience: The Biology of the Mind

Ward — The Student’s Guide to Cognitive Neuroscience

Purves et al. — Neuroscience

Bear, Connors & Paradiso — Neuroscience: Exploring the Brain

Teaching Format:

Tutorials are delivered in a small-group format and focus on:

Discussion of tutorial essays

Critical evaluation of key experiments and theories

Interpretation of neuropsychological and neuroimaging evidence

knowledge testing

Examination-style questions and essay planning
