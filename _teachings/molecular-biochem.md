## Course Overview

These tutorials develop a mechanistic understanding of molecular biochemistry through
discussion-based teaching, problem-solving, and analysis of past Oxford examination questions.

The course emphasises:

- First-principles reasoning in molecular biology and biochemistry
- Integration of structural, biochemical, and cellular mechanisms
- Interpretation of experimental evidence
- Development of concise, high-level exam answers
- Critical evaluation of canonical biological models

## Topics Covered

- DNA replication and repair
- Transcription and genome regulation
- Genome architecture and transposable elements
- Biological membranes and membrane dynamics
- Lipids and membrane fluidity
- Cell signalling pathways
- GPCR and RTK signalling
- Second messenger systems
- Signal amplification and regulation

## Recommended Textbooks

- Alberts et al. — *Molecular Biology of the Cell*
- Berg, Tymoczko & Stryer — *Biochemistry*
- Lodish et al. — *Molecular Cell Biology*

## Teaching Format

Tutorials are delivered in a small-group format and focus on:

- Powerpoint quizzing
- Mechanistic explanation
- Examination-style workthroughs
