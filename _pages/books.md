        <p>
          Why do our brains age differently? How do the
          experiences of a lifetime shape our brain health?
        </p>

        <p>
          <em>Neuroepigenetics: How We Live Shapes How We Age</em>
          explores how the environments we inhabit,
          the experiences we accumulate, and the
          biological processes they influence contribute
          to individual differences in brain and
          cognitive ageing.
        </p>

        <p>
          Bringing together neuroscience, epigenetics,
          epidemiology, and the science of ageing,
          the book examines how our lived experiences
          become biologically embedded across the
          life course.
        </p>

        <p>
          The book is currently under contract with
          Oxford University Press.
        </p>
